A curated list of resources for the **2026 TOEFL iBT** — the redesigned test with a new 1–6 score scale, 11 new task types, and adaptive Reading and Listening modules.

> Maintained by the [ToeflHub](https://toeflhub.com) team. ToeflHub entries are our own platform and are marked as such. No affiliate links.

## Official — ETS

- [TOEFL iBT — ETS](https://www.ets.org/toefl) — Official TOEFL homepage: test info, registration, and preparation.
- [Understanding your TOEFL scores — ETS](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html) — The official 1–6 scale and score conversion table.
- [TOEFL TestReady — ETS](https://testready.ets.org) — ETS's official free prep portal with practice tests and personalized study plans.
